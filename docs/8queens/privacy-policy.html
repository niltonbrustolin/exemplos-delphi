Privacy Policy — 8 Queens

Last updated: September 29, 2026 · Português · Español

This policy describes how the 8 Queens app ("the app"), developed by Nilton Brustolin, handles user information.

Data stored by the app

The app stores your game progress (solutions found, best times, stars, hints and the selected theme) only on your device. This data is not sent to us or to third parties and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

We do not ask for sign-up, name, e-mail or any personal data.

In-app purchases

Purchases (remove ads and themes) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment data; the app only records on the device which items were purchased.

Ads

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • the device advertising identifier (advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device information and diagnostics and performance data;
  • interactions with ads.

This processing is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where required by applicable law (such as the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version will always be available at this address.

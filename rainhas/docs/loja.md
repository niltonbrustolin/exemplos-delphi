# English (en-US)

## App name (max 30)

8 Queens: 3D Chess Puzzles

## Short description (max 80)

Eight queens and the knight's tour in 3D. Logic puzzles with chess pieces!

## Full description

Can you place 8 queens on a chessboard so that none of them attack each
other? Mathematicians and programmers have been fascinated by this puzzle
since 1848 — Gauss himself studied it. And can a knight visit every square of
the board exactly once? Euler worked on that one.

Now it's your turn — in 3D!

♛ QUEENS — CLASSIC MODE
Boards from 4×4 to 12×12. The 8×8 alone has 92 solutions. How many can you
find?

♛ QUEENS — CHALLENGES
72 levels with some queens already placed and only ONE possible solution.

♞ KNIGHT'S TOUR
Jump in an "L" and visit every square exactly once. Harder levels have
blocked squares in your way.

♞ PEACEFUL KNIGHTS
Place as many knights as possible with no knight attacking another — and
watch out: the obvious trick won't work!

📅 DAILY CHALLENGE
A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Keep your streak alive and
earn bonus hints.

✦ BEAUTIFUL 3D BOARD
Rotate the board with your finger, pinch to zoom, and watch the pieces drop
and jump into place. Choose themes like Wood, Tournament, Marble and Neon.

✦ MADE TO LEARN AND HAVE FUN
• 130+ levels with stars and progress
• Smart hints when you get stuck
• Conflicting pieces turn red
• Undo button
• Works offline

Great for training logical thinking, focus and strategy — for chess players,
students, teachers and puzzle lovers.
